Frequently asked questions

How does cloud backup work?

Your save is encrypted on your phone with a key that never leaves your device, then backed up to the developer's server. The server stores only unreadable data and cannot see your food, weight, or health information. Cloud backup is on by default and can be turned off in Settings.

How do I restore my backup on a new phone?

When you open Boneheadz Gym on a new phone, the welcome screen includes a "Restore a backup" option. Follow that path and your encrypted backup will be pulled and unlocked on your new device.

What is the step race?

The step race ranks players by their weekly step count. Steps are counted by your phone via Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android). Your real activity earns XP and progression in the Boneyard.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings and select "Delete account & cloud data". Confirm by typing DELETE. This removes your cloud account, friend connections, step race entry and encrypted backup immediately, and wipes the local save on this phone as part of the same action. (The separate "Erase" control wipes only this phone's save and keeps the cloud account.)
