## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored in one place, generic everywhere else.** 60.4% of the visible stage is flat untouched ground (per-row modal colour, tolerance 7/255), 68.4% of the right half. Mean stage colour rgb(101,109,71), a mid-green field inside an app whose sheet ground measures rgb(22,21,29). The haunted register (keeper, crossed-bone compost, crow, bone by the shed) is under 16% of stage area, and its largest piece is cropped on a 667-tall phone. Swap the skeleton for a farmer and the bone-coin for a gold coin and a generic cozy farm ships this backdrop unchanged.

What could not be shipped by anyone else is the writing. `hlwLine` reads from the same garden object the beds render from, so the keeper cannot assert what the screen contradicts; `S.hlwSalt` holds the pick stable for the visit; the name lands in about a third of the pool and nameless players get the rest. That is authored. It is also 47px tall in a 710px scene.

Deterministic scan: 6 `broken-image` hits on `js/app.js`, **all six false positives**, re-verified line by line rather than inherited: every one is the literal string `<img>` inside a prose comment. The detector has no comment-stripping pass. The previous `single-font` hit on `index.html` is **gone**, confirmed in the render (`document.fonts.check('16px Bangers')` true, `--display` resolves to Bangers, five of nine on-screen text elements paint in it). Zero true positives across four files.

## What's Working

1. **The keeper's voice, and the fact that it structurally cannot lie.** Eight pools gated on state the garden already knows, nameless variants so an unregistered player still gets a talking keeper, and a per-visit salt so he does not reshuffle while you sit there. Both independent reviewers named this the single best asset and both warned that a later pass will be tempted to turn the bubble into a status readout because it is the biggest object on screen. Do not.
2. **Accent discipline, measured.** Exactly one accent object on the returning screen (THIRSTY chip, 1,010 css px², 12.26:1 against its own ink) and two on the first visit. Lime is 0.31% of pixels returning, 1.84% first visit. The accent means "this one" and nothing else. Any hierarchy fix must MOVE the accent, never add a second.
3. **The keeper stands on grass**, verified across all three bands: no panel, no card, no border, contact shadow landing on the lawn. This is what makes the diorama read as one continuous world.
4. **The 740-unit stage call was right.** maxScroll is 48px at 390x844, one viewport with a path running off the bottom edge.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The payoff action is the least visible thing on the screen, and the upsell is the loudest.**
Both reviewers found this independently, by different measurements. A: the ripe fruit is 175 css px² at 1.81:1 against grass, last by area AND last by contrast, while the OPTIONAL watering action gets a 64.7 x 21.3 accent chip; a ready bed carries no chip at all. The director seeded the state players actually live in (all beds growing, nothing to touch, which nobody had reviewed because nobody seeds it) and measured the 1,500 plaque at 4,361 px of cream at 4.44:1 against every living crop on screen combined at 162 px at 2.98:1: **26.9x the visual area of the entire garden's plant life**.
Fix: move the accent to the ready bed (a READY chip in the slot the time chip uses), demote thirst to the muted chip with the droplet, and make the ghost slot actually ghostly: dashed ink on bare soil, no cream card, no hard shadow.
Suggested command: /impeccable colorize

**[P1] The payoff MOMENT is broken three ways, and every guard on this screen is blind to it.**
Found only by the director, who fired a real harvest tap and measured at t+1400ms. The Impeccable detector pass found none of it because it measured the screen at rest, and its own clean sheet is the proof: 324 hit probes, zero mismatches, zero overlaps, all static.
- The keeper occludes **80.6 x 57.5px of an 84 x 60 bed**: 96% of the width and 96% of the height of the thing you just harvested. The pluck, the fruit arc and the empty-bed reveal all play behind a skeleton.
- His speech bubble is **clipped 23.0px at 390x844 and 22.1px at 375x667, 11.4% of it**, with 15px off the device viewport entirely. It fires on any left-column bed, which is three of five positions.
- The "+3 Ember Pepper" reward toast **collides with a neighbouring timer chip by 13.2 x 14.8px**.
Fix: use the stand-beside offset the designer already specced for watering (-74x) on harvest too, clamp the bubble rect to the stage, offset the toast off the chip row. Then add a MOTION section to `tests/hollow-audit.mjs` that fires a real tap and asserts rects at t+400/1400/2400ms.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] The modal does not contain focus, and Escape does not close it.**
Measured by B: the tab cycle is **59 stops long and the first stop inside the Hollow is stop 46**. Stops 0 to 45 are all behind the modal (hero buttons, wallet pills, nav tabs, the Kitchen sheet's own controls), invisible under the sheet. `.sheet` carries `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal` null, no accessible name, `inert` false, and 45 background focusables still tabbable. Escape pressed with focus inside leaves the sheet present 700ms later. All seven controls do have a visible focus ring, but it is the unstyled Chromium default (`outline: auto 1px rgb(0,95,204)`), and `app.css` has zero `:focus-visible` rules.
Fix: move focus into the sheet on open, `aria-modal="true"`, name the dialog, mark the background inert, restore focus on close, wire Escape, and add a brand `:focus-visible` ring that survives on transparent `.hlw-bed` buttons. App-wide, but it lands hardest here because every control on this screen is an invisible box.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P1] The time-of-day system produces almost nothing.** Both reviewers, different bands, same conclusion. Director: night is only **16% darker than day and identical in hue**, mean absolute difference [13.2, 14.0, 3.4] per channel; you cannot tell the two renders apart without the fireflies. A: mean stage luminance day 104.8, **dusk 104.1**, night 88.5, so dusk is a hue rotation with no perceptible change and a third of the clock returns nothing. The .42-to-.22 fix earlier today was right that .42 was muddy, but it treated "too dark" and "reads as night" as the same dial.
Fix: keep the alpha low, change hue and LOCAL contrast instead. Cool the grass toward blue-green, warm and widen the lantern pool so it has something to push against, brighten the fireflies. Night is not the same picture dimmer; night is the light coming from one place.
Suggested command: /impeccable colorize

**[P2] The 1,500 sign prices an object that is not drawn.** `js/hollow-beds.js` ships a `hollow-bed-locked` ghost-bed piece and `openHollow` never renders it. The sign hangs over bare grass. On a true first visit the upsell is on screen before the player has planted a seed, competing with the FREE STARTER SEEDS cue. The armed pill covers bed2 and the sign it is confirming, and the refusal arrives after the second tap. Two coin glyphs for one currency, 274px apart. The price is set in -apple-system 16px while the chip 100px away is Bangers 12px, and the designer's spec says Bangers for signs.
Fix: draw the locked bed under the sign, show `340 / 1,500` on the board when short, move the armed pill above the sign, use the app's own coin glyph, set the price in Bangers.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Crop identity never reaches the screen.** Six of seven crops are one tinted silhouette, and at seed/sprout/young the tintable fruit does not exist, so two different crops differ by 22/255 mean pixel value. The chip carries time only. With three to five beds a player cannot tell what is growing, which is the exact information that brings them back for a specific bed.
Fix: put the name in the chip until Cam draws more crops. Cheap, and it also gives the ready state a chip, which fixes half of the first P1.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

## Where the two reviews disagree

One direct conflict, worth recording. On the dead right half (60.4% flat ground, and a measured 157 x 223 css px region with 0.0% drawn content), Assessment A's preferred fix is the comp's second plot with beds 4 and 5 in it. The director explicitly rejects that: ten beds is more game than a calorie tracker's side dish should carry, it doubles the timer, art and balance surface, and set dressing is a one-time cost where beds are a forever cost. The director's reading also matches the shipped economy (PLOTS_MAX = 5). Recommendation: fill the space with world, not mechanics.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer).** Gets three agreeing instructions on the first visit, then the fourth object in his eye path is a cream board reading 1,500 hanging over grass with no bed under it. The three brown ovals have no labels. Nothing names a crop. "Cauldrons" refers to a system he has not met.

**Casey (one-handed, interrupted).** 2.9px gutter between bed2 and a 1,500-coin purchase at 390x844, 2.8px at 375x667. No overlap, so every automated check passes, but a moving thumb on a 57.5px target will arm the spend, and the armed pill then lands on top of the bed that armed it. The compost heap is 40% visible at 390x844 and entirely below the fold at 375x667, needing 196px of scroll. Every action costs a 0.9-1.2s walk during which taps are dropped silently.

**Sam (screen reader, keyboard).** Cannot enter the sheet without tabbing 45 invisible background controls. The bed aria-labels are genuinely good and name the crop the visual never does. But the keeper's `role="status" aria-live="polite"` node is recreated by a wholesale `innerHTML` on an unconditional 30-second interval, so the same joke is likely re-announced every 30 seconds for the entire visit. `aria-hidden` count inside the sheet is 0, so 27 pieces of decorative SVG and two steam wisps are exposed.

## Minor Observations

- The lantern's flame and lit glass draw in every band and the post carries an unconditional warm drop-shadow. It burns at noon.
- The Hollow drops `res.bumper` entirely, so the lucky +1 roll is indistinguishable from an ordinary pick. The list version of the same action fires a 26-particle confetti burst for it. The prettier surface has the flatter payoff.
- The decorative steam wisp fails contrast in all three bands (3.13 / 3.44 / 3.94 : 1 against 4.5). It is decoration, not copy, so the right fix is `aria-hidden` and accepting it, not recolouring it.
- `P.note` at 5.00:1 is the thinnest real-copy margin on the screen.
- `.sheet-close` measures 55.8 x 44.0, exactly on the 44px floor with zero margin.
- The compost button is still named `#hlwCrow` although it now targets the compost heap.
- The returning instruction copy contains an em dash, against the project's own standing rule, in shipping copy.
- Clean and worth stating: zero page errors and zero console errors across 19 runs, zero overlaps, zero hit-test mismatches across 324 probes, zero tap targets under 44px, zero animations running under reduced motion with a measured 0.000% frame delta confirming it, and no layout shift.

## Questions to Consider

1. The keeper is the only authored thing on the screen and he is furniture. What if he were the interface: he walks to the ripe bed on entry and points, and the chip disappears? The hierarchy problem and the specificity problem have the same fix.
2. `openGardenSheet` still ships the whole garden loop as a list, with a better harvest payoff. Which one is the product? Two UIs for one system is why the peak regressed.
3. On a rest day with nothing ripe and no seeds, the screen offers one joke and no action. What is the reward for that visit? The stated ambition is not funded by anything currently on screen.
4. Is a 1,500-coin bed the right thing to sell on the screen whose job is to be worth opening on a day you did not walk, in the accent colour, before the player has harvested once?
5. Every guard on this screen asserts on a still frame. What else in this app is correct at rest and broken in motion?
