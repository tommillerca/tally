# Boneheadz Gym — Android app install (for Brock)

Hey! Here's the real Android app so your steps finally count (pets level from walking, step quests, energy from walks — none of that works in the Chrome version). Your level 18 progress comes with you. Takes about 3 minutes.

## Step 1 — Save your progress FIRST (in Chrome, before anything else)

1. Open the game in Chrome like usual
2. Go to **Settings** (bottom right)
3. Tap **Export** and save the file (it lands in your Downloads)

Don't skip this. This file IS your account — level, gear, pets, friends, everything.

## Step 2 — Install the app

1. Open the **BoneheadzGym-brock.apk** file Tom sent you (tap it in your Downloads)
2. Android will warn about installing unknown apps — allow it for whatever app you opened it from (Chrome/Files). It's Tom's own signed build, not a store app yet.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **Boneheadz Gym**

## Step 3 — Bring your progress in

1. In the new app, get past the first screen (put anything in, it gets replaced)
2. Go to **Settings → Import** and pick the file from Step 1
3. You should be level 18 with all your stuff, same friends, same name

## Step 4 — Turn on step tracking (the whole point)

1. **Settings → Connect Health** (or the steps card on the home screen)
2. Allow **Steps** and **Active calories** when Android asks
3. If your phone is on Android 13 or older, install the free **Health Connect** app from the Play Store first (Android 14+ has it built in)

Go for a walk, your pet levels up now. 💀

## If anything looks wrong

Don't play on. Tell Tom, your export file + a cloud backup both exist, nothing is lost.

After you're in the app, stop using the Chrome version so your progress doesn't split between the two.
