How this avoids paywalled kindness

  • The words are free. Premium changes the frame, never the vocabulary. All twelve phrases stay available to everyone, so nobody can buy a thing to say.
  • The free cheer is not made worse. Same size, same slot, same reply button, no "basic" styling and no cooldown a paid cheer skips.
  • Sending costs the same. One shared daily cap per friend, enforced on the server, identical for both.
  • The receiver's game does not move. No coins, no XP, no dust, no streak. A cheer pays nothing today and a premium one must keep paying nothing, or flair has become power.
  • Replies are symmetric. A free cheer back lands in a paying player's inbox exactly as a paid one lands in a free player's.
  • Nothing is gated behind being liked. Premium is bought by the SENDER for their own outgoing cheers. It is never earned by receiving them, so being popular is never the price.
