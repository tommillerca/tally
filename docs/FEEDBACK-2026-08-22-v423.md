## 1. The Wanderer's cone  [OPEN, three separate faults]

> "the wanderer's light cone is flickering in size and still pointing strangely
> out of the lamp. Currently the cone shoots down and to the left of him it
> should just be straight out horizontally in the same direction he is facing.
> Th cone shouldn't flicker or change size"

Three causes, found:

- **1a FLICKER.** `@keyframes wandererLantern` animates opacity .74 to .98 on a
  3.2s infinite loop. I added that as a lantern breathing. He reads it as a
  fault, so it goes.
- **1b SIZE JITTER.** v423 bound `sizeWandererCones` to `map.on('move')` as well
  as `zoom` to fix his drift. That repaints on every move frame with sub-pixel
  differences, so the cone now shivers. Round it and only repaint on a real
  change.
- **1c DIRECTION, and this one has a consequence.** The cone is drawn at his TRUE
  compass heading, which is any of 360 degrees. His ART has only TWO facings
  (mirrored east or west, v423). So on a south-west beat the light correctly
  points down-left while the man is drawn facing left, and it reads as broken.

  **Tom wants the cone horizontal, matching the facing. The catch test must move
  with it or the light lies about where you get caught.** `inWandererCone`
  compares a real bearing against `w.heading`, so if only the DRAWING is
  horizontalised, players get caught by light that is not on screen. Whatever is
  drawn must be what catches. Flagged to Tom.

## 2. A reset-gear button in the Wardrobe  [OPEN, player suggestion]

> "We had a player suggest a good idea of having a button in wardrobe that fully
> resets your bonehead's gear so it's easier to make a new outfit from scratch"

Not a bug. Strip every slot in one action so a new outfit starts from nothing.
Note what it must NOT do: this is cosmetics, not inventory. It unequips; it never
deletes, sells or refunds anything.

## 3. "Not right now" reads as part of the creatures banner  [OPEN]

> "For some reason it says 'not right now' right above the banner for the new
> creatures"

Not orphaned code, a GROUPING problem. `js/app.js:3552` puts the `.ul-skip`
button inside `.ul-wrap` under the "Start with breakfast" nudge, and the hype
banner is the very next block. `.ul-skip` is `align-self: center` with
`padding: 7px 12px 2px`, so it sits closer to the banner below it than to the
card it belongs to. It reads as the banner's dismiss.

## 4. Tab switching is still clunky  [OPEN]

> "Overall performance wise the app is very clunky switching between tabs is not
> smooth it's showing a staggered preview of the the existing page as you swap"

**"A staggered preview of the existing page" is the specific symptom and it is
new information.** The OLD screen is still on display while the new one
assembles, in pieces. v423 fixed the art re-scan (2.8M pixels per hub arrival)
and the DB waterfall (42 transactions to 31), and both moved real numbers, but
neither addressed what the player SEES during the swap.

Look at `revealWhenReady` and `route()`: the reveal holds the new screen until
its images decode, so what is on screen in the meantime is the previous one. The
fix is about the HANDOVER, not about more speed. v422's route-flash work is the
nearest prior art, and its lesson applies: grade PIXELS across the transition,
not timings.
