# v474: the Locker Room opens, and the rack costs more

## Summary

- Ships the football kit live on its own Shop shelf, the Locker Room: five garments
  (helmet, jersey, cleats, a matching lizard helmet and jersey), each sold once for
  4,200 coins and worn in all 32 invented team colours; the bundle is 16,800 and,
  per Tom's 2026-09-05 critique, now charges only for whatever pieces you don't
  already own instead of the flat price on top of what you paid separately.
- Generalises the football kit's colourway rail: Collection and Stable now collapse
  every colourway family (football's 32 teams, any hand-drawn recolour series) into
  one tile per drawing with a count badge, closing the memory-census overage those
  screens picked up from one-`<img>`-per-colourway rendering
  (wardrobe-family-audit.mjs, wardrobe-family-grid-audit.mjs, memory-census.mjs).
- Economy pass: the cosmetic rack roughly doubles (coins and Bone Dust, in
  lockstep so every stated ratio holds), with the very first Common purchase held
  at its old price so a new player's first weeks are unaffected. Crates can now
  roll a duplicate at the rolled rarity instead of always avoiding owned pieces,
  and a logged-but-off-budget day no longer carries a bonus crate (XP unchanged).
- Five player-visible fixes folded in from the day's QA rounds: a returning
  player's day-close reward now pays even when the day-guard witness check is
  still in flight (lapse-witness-audit.mjs); a cloud restore can no longer
  resurrect an opened crate or refund spent coins on a second boot
  (restore-latch-audit.mjs); the weekly step race now pays the settler who just
  rolled into a new week instead of overwriting their own row before the
  payout query runs (server-side, see Deploy below); the Pit's move tray shows
  its cost on its own line instead of wrapping the hint and losing a row
  (fight-tray-audit.mjs, fight-hint-audit.mjs); and the dead-shell watchdog no
  longer reloads a shell that is merely slow to download (dead-shell-audit.mjs).
- ~96 gate-registered guards run in `tests/release-gate.mjs` (41 in the PURE
  tier, which runs on every gate); `node tests/unit.test.js` is 285/0 on this
  tree.
