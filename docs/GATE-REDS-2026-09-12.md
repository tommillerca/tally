## Common currency-fixture mechanism

`js/app.js:28094` initializes the demo with `coinsAdd(340)`. That writer (`js/loot.js:819`) records balance/revision/history through `js/db.js:920`. The test helper instead writes just `coins` and `bonedust` at `tests/godmode.js:937` through `:943`. Plain `kvSet` also delegates to `db.put` (`js/db.js:998`, `:968`) without currency recording. The next revisioned mutation rejects mismatched history at `js/db.js:526`; export rejects it at `:1402`.

The missing operation is not necessarily creation of a first history row: the damaging case is retaining an existing history whose total no longer equals the newly seeded balance. An absent history is deliberately accepted as a legacy opening at `js/db.js:525`. Therefore the three dust-only Wardrobe seeds remain unresolved rather than being counted automatically in the coin cluster.

A Node control using the real database and wallet exports over `tests/mem-idb.mjs` produced:

```text
PASS raw balance overwrite after coinsAdd(340) makes the next coinsAdd reject with the supplied currency-history error
PASS revisioned delta to 1000 preserves history: next credit reaches 1001 and export succeeds
PASS absent dust history accepts a legacy opening balance: raw dust seed alone does not establish a crash
```

These controls model the source mechanism, not the browser boot schedule. A safe proposal retains the webdriver/demo checks and uses deltas through the production currency writer after boot settles, then validates the fixture before any product assertion. Do not weaken the product history check or silently delete a player's history.
